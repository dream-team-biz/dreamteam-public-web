Anatomy of one task

One real feature, from an approved plan to merged code: what the AI agents built and checked, my decisions as the engineer, and what it took in time and money.

The task taught our task board to say why one task waits for another. It was built, reviewed, tested and merged.

  • 20 h 25 min elapsed, from the first step to the merge
  • at least $163 at Claude API list prices
  • about $5 on our Claude subscription
  • 1–2 hours of human time

Claude API list prices: what this work costs bought by the token. Claude Team subscription: the flat price that makes it about $5 — this task's share of one seat's week. Dream Team's cost optimization records every step's tokens and time and gives each step the model its job needs. We keep optimizing the process to do more work for the same money.

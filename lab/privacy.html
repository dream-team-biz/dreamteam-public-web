Dream Team Lab counts visits here with Google Analytics

It runs only on the Dream Team Lab pages, and on a computer you can turn it off with Google's opt-out add-on.

It records the pages you open and some of your actions on them. Actions include reaching the bottom of a page and clicking a link to another site. It also records the page that sent you here, and your browser and device.

Google estimates a rough location from your IP address.

Its cookies are for these pages only. They are first-party: small files your browser keeps, set by these pages. Their names start with “_ga”. Your browser can block or delete them.

Google says its opt-out add-on stops Google Analytics from using your visits. Get it for Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge on a computer.

When your browser loads these pages, two other services receive your IP address. Google Fonts serves the fonts from Google's servers. GitHub Pages, our hosting, serves the pages.

Questions go to the Talk to us page. Its form is delivered by Un-static, a form service; we use what you send only to answer you.

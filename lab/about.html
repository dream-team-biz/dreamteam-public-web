Two founders, both hands-on

DreamTeam Lab is a company run by two engineering leaders who build DreamTeam using its own AI agents, which work as a software team with engineers in control.

The founders

The humans on the team

Alex and Boris are the two humans who maintain DreamTeam, and both of them write code for it.

Alex Rapoport

Founder · CEO and CTO of DreamTeam Lab

Alex leads DreamTeam's development and builds it as a developer, working with its agents.

For more than 25 years at Intel (August 2000 to May 2026), he built the processes and platforms other engineers worked on — Git, CI/CD, DevOps, an on-prem AI cloud. He led engineering teams for 12 of those years, grew the team he led, and was most recently an AI Cloud Infrastructure Architect.

“I know how a software team should work. I just never had one that worked exactly the way I wanted. So I built one.”

Boris Daich

Co-founder · COO · Developer

Boris runs operations and builds DreamTeam as a developer; he also works with our early design partners and sets DreamTeam up in their teams.

He started programming at 14, in assembly language, and has since built and led engineering teams, including as CTO and VP R&D of a US logistics startup. He also led a distributed R&D team across four continents and has helped other companies' teams adopt new technology.

“Choose the best people, light a spark in their hearts and then no task is too hard.”

The agents

The rest of the team is AI agents

Nine specialist roles work under an orchestrator, which runs each task's pipeline and dispatches the other agents.

  • orchestrator
  • architect
  • backend developer
  • frontend developer
  • DevOps
  • reviewer
  • tester
  • technical writer
  • presenter
  • archaeologist

The presenter turns finished work into a story of what changed and why, with steps you can check yourself. The archaeologist reads an existing codebase module by module and returns cited notes, which the orchestrator turns into a map.

Engineers stay in control at the decision points of The Dream Team Life Cycle.

How we work

We build DreamTeam with DreamTeam

Instead of adding AI to a process built for people, we build the process around AI: The Dream Team Life Cycle. DreamTeam builds itself almost every day, and it has built other projects from inside its own workshop. Built with itself: about 665 tasks closed in four months. Making DreamTeam work inside anyone's repository is what we are building now; today we set it up ourselves with our early design partners.

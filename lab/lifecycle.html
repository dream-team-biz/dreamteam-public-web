The Dream Team Life Cycle

You decide at set points — the vision, the plan, each task's start and its result — and AI agents do the work in between.

What it is: the method DreamTeam runs — AI agents built for companies with their own engineering team, with the engineers in control. An idea becomes a vision, a plan and tasks; each task is built, reviewed, tested and merged through CI.

Claude API list prices: what this work costs bought by the token. Claude Team subscription: the flat price that makes it about $5 — this task's share of one seat's week. DreamTeam's cost optimization records every step's tokens and time and gives each step the model its job needs. We keep optimizing the process to do more work for the same money.

03 · Where we're going

Dream it. Dream Team it. Ship it.

What we are building toward: human ideas turn into good software almost automatically, with engineers and their team in control.

  1. Dream it. We want any idea to become a plan without rework. Getting from an idea to tasks is the hardest problem we work on.
  2. Dream Team it. We want every engineer to have a dream team: AI agents that do the work, in a process the engineer controls.
  3. Ship it. We want every team to ship good software from its own ideas, with AI agents doing the work between its decisions.

04 · What you can see

Every step records what it took

Results · T-707, 18–19 September 2026

The change: +1,125 lines of code in 6 files, +3,091 lines of tests in 9.

  • 20 h 25 min end to end
  • 10 h 18 min of it, waiting for the engineer's decisions
  • at least $163 at Claude API list prices
  • about $5 Claude Team subscription + DreamTeam cost optimization
  • 1–2 hours of human time

Claude API list prices: the work bought by the token, from the task's record and session transcripts; not yet reconciled with provider invoices. Claude Team subscription: a flat price; this is the task's share of one seat's week. DreamTeam's cost optimization records every step's tokens and time and gives each step the model its job needs.

No DreamTeam server: the task's record lives in your Git. Anatomy of one task: the full record, step by step.

Where each log row and number comes from

Times are UTC, truncated to the minute. The runlog records when each step ended and how long it took; a step's start is its end minus its duration.

The results

  • 20 h 25 min: 73,493 s from the first step, 13:19:28 on 18 September (rollup.json), to the merge (squash-trace.json L120).
  • 10 h 18 min: the waits for the engineer at Init's gate, before Finalize and before Close: 15,465 + 21,336 + 268 s.
  • At least $163: the record's tokens at Claude API list prices (config/models.yaml v2.3), $143.37, plus the contract review recovered from the init transcript, $19.72. A lower bound.

The schema

The stages and the engineer's three points, in DreamTeam's config/workflows.yaml: the readiness decision (L1225–1262), Finalize started by a person (L2775–2781), Close as the approval (L5413). Review and fix: at most 2 rounds, then the task goes to the engineer (L2363–2375). Who worked in T-707's Run: its subagent records.

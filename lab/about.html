We build an AI software team with engineers in control

Dream Team is our team of AI agents, built for companies with their own engineering team. Engineers set the goals, approve the plan and sign off on every result.

01 · Who builds it

Two engineering leaders who still write the code

Alex and Boris are the two humans who maintain Dream Team.

Alex Rapoport

Founder · CEO and CTO of Dream Team Lab

Alex leads Dream Team's development and builds it as a developer, working with its agents.

For more than 25 years at Intel (August 2000 to May 2026), he built the processes and platforms other engineers worked on — Git, CI/CD, DevOps, an on-prem AI cloud. He led engineering teams for 12 of those years, grew the team he led, and was most recently an AI Cloud Infrastructure Architect.

“I know how a software team should work. I just never had one that worked exactly the way I wanted. So I built one.”

Boris Daich

Co-founder · COO · Developer

Boris runs operations and builds Dream Team as a developer; he also works with our early design partners and sets Dream Team up in their teams.

He started programming at 14, in assembly language, and has since built and led engineering teams, including as CTO and VP R&D of a US logistics startup. He also led a distributed R&D team across four continents and has helped other companies' teams adopt new technology.

“Choose the best people, light a spark in their hearts and then no task is too hard.”

03 · Where we're going

Dream it. Dream Team it. Ship it.

What we are building toward: human ideas turn into good software almost automatically, with engineers and their team in control.

  1. Dream it. We want any idea to become a plan without rework. Getting from an idea to tasks is the hardest problem we work on.
  2. Dream Team it. We want every engineer to have a dream team: AI agents that do the work, in a process the engineer controls.
  3. Ship it. We want every team to ship good software from its own ideas, with AI agents doing the work between its decisions.

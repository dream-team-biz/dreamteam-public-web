Results of one real task · Anatomy of one task

The task taught our task board to say why one task waits for another. It was built, reviewed, tested and merged.

  • 20 h 25 min end to end
  • at least $163 at Claude API list prices
  • about $5 on our Claude subscription
  • 1–2 hours of human time
  • 2 blockers from a 22-minute review by 4 agents, before any code

Claude API list prices: what this work costs bought by the token. Claude Team subscription: the flat price that makes it about $5 — this task's share of one seat's week. DreamTeam's cost optimization records every step's tokens and time and gives each step the model its job needs. We keep optimizing the process to do more work for the same money.
